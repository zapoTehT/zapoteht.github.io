The Academy is known as the breeding ground that fuels the spark of our youth. Every year, his Royal Majesty sends his specially trained convey out to the 43 districts of our nation, and through a series of rigourous tests, roughly 1000 exceptional individuals are granted the privilege to enter the Academy.

The new students are split between three separate disciplines. The first of which is Field, where the students are trained in both the theoretical concepts behind nanotech, but also put directly into practice, harnessing our nation's technology to build their repertoire. The next is Parliament. As the name would suggest, these are the individuals that have been selected to ensure that the systems that keep order within our nation remain intact as the generations are passed down. The final is Research. This discipline interacts directly with the other two, both looking to develop new technology for the field but also to use their minds to develop new technologies to improve the lives of the citizens of our nation.

\- A snippet from Chapter 3 of Imperial History

Despite the feverish excitement he had been feeling mere days ago, Gerald could not stop himself from yawning. It had been a long journey. He had set foot from Toronia to the Academy three days ago, accompanied by three others who had been selected like him. They were escorted by members of the Imperial Guard, where they made the two day hike to a neighbouring town. There, smaller groups of new students gathered where they waited for their transport to the Academy.

Fighting back against his lethargy, Gerald willed a smile onto his face. It was a sight that shattered the expectations built from an entire childhood of fantasticating about his dream finally coming true. When he closed his eyes his feelings of shock and bewilderment sprung back into his mindscape as if they were completely new. The sound of the engine blasting down as the warship slowly descended down from the heavens rang vividly in his ears. It was like a sight from another world. The ship slowly reached ground level like a pebble slowly descending down a body of honey. The neon lights, the slick design, he had only had the opportunity to see a warship in the sky before.

Unfortunately, Gerald had not had much opportunity to see it after boarding. Security on the ship was at an all time high. He had remembered seeing a case on the holonet where a foreign terrorist had snuck onto a Academy-faring warship several years ago and had managed to take the entire thing down. As such, it was pivotal that all students remained locked down in their own individual cabinets with only a restroom and 9 squared meters of walking room for him to try to entertain himself with. Even though he was trying to keep his spirits up, the hours rolled upon each other until the roar of the engine dying signaled that his boredom was finally coming to an end.

"Field students, await to be escorted!" A voice hollered over the intercom system.

Gerald grabbed his backpack and after ensuring that all of his closest personal belongings hadn't magically teleported away, laced his boots back on and stood ready at guard at the door. A minute later, the door opened and Gerald was greeted by a silver hulking figure.

"Follow me."

Gerald complied. They walked in silence for a few minutes before they arrived at the cargo hull. What would normally contain boxes upon boxes of munitions and supplies had been cleared out. Neon lights lined the exit door and a purple carpet had been lined down from the point where they had all gathered to the point where the hull opens. The guardsmen, after having escorted their individual students to this room, had taken position around the carpet, like an insulating layer of a house. Gerald began to think about the fact that this was a bit too dramatic when suddenly the neon lights closed and the cranking of the hull revealed the light hidden behind.

And just like that Gerald's world had finally come together.

"Welcome children," a calm soothing voice pierced through the room. "I am sure you all already know who I am, but for the sake of formalities let me introduce myself. I am Principal Chalmers and welcome to the Imperial Academy."

The hull had finally and the backdrop of the Academy finally graced his eyes. The first thing he noticed was the magnetic rippling in the sky, marking the protective dome around the Academy. He had only really heard of this in writing, but the distorted oscillations of shades of light blue could only really be described as alien.

The next thing he noticed - although upon later reflection it really should have been the first - was the sight of sweat and joy in front of him. He could only really suspect that the hull had landed in front of one of the training zones and that older years of Field students were here to put on a show. There was a hurricane of colour formed from the blacks from the uniform and the iconic light purple fuzz of the nanotech blades. 

Beyond that, he could make out the towering sight of the idle mecha units that he had only really seen before in the sky and on holonet and the even more towering sight of what he suspected was the Field campus building. 

"You all have been gathered here today as a part of a remarkable group. Year after year, brave talented individuals like yourself are selected to bring his Majesty's light forward to the new generation. In this dark times, your growth and development will be the spark that illluminates this wretched dark world." 

Principal Chalmers paused for a second to bask in the youthful reverberance of his crowd. His eyes danced from corner to corner. There was not one person who was giving him their fullest attention.

"So let us begin by giving our thanks to his Majesty," he continued. "May the Trussian Light guide us all."

Gerald placed his right hand to his heart and raised his left hand to the sky. It was a gesture and a statement he had known for his entire life.

"May the Trussian Light guide us all."
