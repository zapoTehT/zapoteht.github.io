The turn of the 18th year since the Imperial Palace had established control marked the end of the era of turmoil within the nation. 

On the 5th day before the Tides of Ede, the unnamed rebel leader was brought to trial in the Imperial Capital. Thousands gathered in person and millions other witnessed the event through holoair. His crimes were numerous; the people of our nation were shocked to know that what they knew was only the surface of the iceberg. There was no room for questions or bargaining. The world needed this man gone.

Not much is known about the leader of the insurgents. Some rumours state that he was born of demon blood, and the mere act of meeting his gaze would be enough to infect someone to rid them of their sanity. Other rumours state that he was a master of the mind arts and the army he had gathered was the result of a mass brainwashing. It seems that we will never learn the truth behind his identity.

Thus the soft thud of his head rolling down the execution platform marked the end of the struggle of power between the Imperial Palace and the insurgents. Without their leader, the craze spread through our people died out and the world returned to a safer time.

A young man awoke to the presence of light. His heart jolted in surprise - it had been weeks since the door to the outside world had been opened. The sound of chains ringing oscillated through the narrow corridor, illuminating the figure who stood at the doorway. Even in his delirious state he knew who had come to visit him. Afterall, no amounts of starvation of lethargy could make him forget the silhouette of the man who defined his childhood.

"Mark, you still alive?" the man asked as he walked closer and closer. Even the sound of silence seemed to not want to respond to this question.

"Hey, I know you're still alive. Look, its me Gerald. You can talk to me," he continued. "We keep track of your vitals. After all the Palace wouldn't want their golden egg to die on them."

"Gerald Truss." Mark noted, seemingly finally out of his stupor. His words came out slurred, his voice ragged and harsh. 

"Look what they've done to you." Gerald remarked. 

Mark had always been big for his age. Growing up he had been bigger and stronger than his peers; his eyes used to be vibrant and hopeful. Yet when Gerald glanced at him now, one would be remiss to think that Gerald was anything but the sort. The faint traces of muscle lined his boney wiry frame only extenuated by the size of the holes for his arms and his legs from the rags covering his body. Red specked chains bound both his hands and legs surrounded by dried red stains which bore the weight of his stuggles.

"No, I should say look what you've done to yourself."

Gerald reached out of his pocket and grabbed a keycard. He walked up to the door to Mark's cell and by placing the card to a stand the door opened. Mark stared at Gerald incredulously.

"This isn't your twenty year old late turn heel moment is it old man?" Mark laughed before coughing violently.

Gerald waited for the silence to creep back before responding. "Would it make you feel any better if I said it was?"

"Nah, definitely not. You're not a liar. Even then, they'd kill both of us by sunrise. I'd rather only one of us die even if its me and not your dumbass."

With a sigh, Gerald sat down onto the floor cross-legged. The floor was cold. It was like this ill-advised meeting was happening above one of frozen lakes deep in winter of their childhood home. But no, the truth was in fact much harsher. This facility existed deep within the surface of the planet, reinforced with maximum security to ensure that no attempts to free the prisoner could even start to be conceived. 

He reached into the inner pockets of his trench coat and pulled out two small glasses and an a gourd. 

"Do you want a drink?" Gerald asked.

"I still remember when you got this for my 18th. It's a shame we never got to drink it together." Mark responded. 

"We are now." Gerald retorted while pouring the alcohol into the glasses. As a symbolic gesture, Gerald placed one glass before him and one before Mark. A small gleam of light flickered in Mark's eyes, and a smile crept to his mouth.

"Can you baby bird that glass to me?" Mark quipped.

Gerald didn't warrant giving that an answer. Instead he flipped his head back and downed the shot of liquor before him. His heart was filled with the warmth both from the fiery liquid as well as the nostalgic springtime memories from his youth.  

"Do you regret anything?" Gerald asked, breaking himself from his fleeting moment of happiness and back to reality.

"How could I?" Mark responded.

Gerald raised the second glass up to the lips of Mark. Mark was never a drinker - a fact that Gerald knew all too well. As such he gently tipped the glass so that the liquid would gently caress the lips of the prisoner. Mark swallowed and gagged, the coughs from earlier returning in stride.

"Fuck, that tastes vile." Mark remarked.  "You reckon its because my tastebuds are fucked or do you sick fucks actually enjoy drinking stuff like that?"

"If you had my taste in alcohol maybe things could've gone differently." Gerald's response cut seemingly only made the silence in the room more oppressive. Minutes passed before Gerald finally gathered the courage to say what he had come here for.

"I'm sorry." 

"Don't be." Mark lamented. "You made your decisions and I made mine. As much as I hate you you're the reason why I'm even here. You taught me how to see things, how to think deeper. I don't know how you manage to keep it in, I know we think the same."

"I didn't want you to do this."

"Well I've always been a problem child," said the captive. "The world is fucked. I know it. You know it. The people out there, they know it as well. I'm just the one out of the two of us with the courage to do anything about it."

"And where did that courage get you?" Gerald screamed. "They're going to kill you and destroy everything you've built." 

"And so what? I'd rather die honouring my values than to snivel up as an old man." Mark retorted.

Gerald did not respond. The sound of his cup getting filled with another round of alcohol echoed through the room. He slowly sipped his drink and when there was no more liquid he took a deep breath he placed the cups back into his trench coat, picked the bottle up and stood up.

"Your execution is scheduled for two hours time." Gerald stated.

"Stand tall. Be proud. They'll hate you for that for as long as our society remains intact."

__________________________________________________________________________

Five hours later a young man stood tall and proud at the sight of his execution. And with a smile he released the gag bounding his ultimate weapon and touched the heart of the thousands watching him for the final time.

No such footage or images exist of this tremendous event in world history.
